05 / HOW MOOLARIS WORKSCLARITY AT EVERY STEP

Understand first.
Then move forward.

Each conversation starts with the specific need. Project scope, training format, and outcomes depend on the requirement and agreed approach.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Hear the challenge, context, and desired direction.

  2. 02

    Understand the need

    Clarify constraints, users, tools, and priorities.

  3. 03

    Propose an approach

    Discuss a suitable scope and possible next step.

  4. 04

    Train or support project learning

    Work through the agreed training or college-project support plan.

  5. 05

    Demonstrate progress

    Review what has been done and decide what follows.
