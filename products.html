02 / PRODUCT JOURNEYA FRAMEWORK, NOT A LIVE STATUS REPORT

From a real need
to a tested direction.

These are possible stages in the product journey. A stage is marked active only when Moolaris confirms progress on a specific product.

  1. 01 / STAGE

    Research

    Understand the need, users, existing options, and context.

  2. 02 / STAGE

    Concept

    Shape a possible solution around clear requirements.

  3. 03 / STAGE

    Prototype

    Where scope allows, build a model to explore how an idea works.

  4. 04 / STAGE

    Validate

    Use tests and feedback to learn what should change or continue.

  5. 05 / STAGE

    Market

    Explore user fit and commercial readiness only when evidence supports it.

✳ Not every idea will reach every stage. Decisions depend on evidence, feasibility, and the specific opportunity.
