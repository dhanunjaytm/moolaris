05 / HOW A PROJECT WORKSA PRACTICAL LEARNING SEQUENCE

Make the work
visible as you learn.

This is a general project structure. Moolaris helps students explore and develop prototypes through final-year project support. Guidance, facilities, deliverables, and project scope depend on confirmed capacity and the agreed enquiry; a particular outcome is not guaranteed.

  1. 01

    Choose a problem

    Describe a need, user, or process worth understanding.

  2. 02

    Plan the build

    Set a question, requirements, and a realistic scope.

  3. 03

    Develop

    Apply relevant skills to a suitable design or build task.

  4. 04

    Test

    Check behavior against the question and record observations.

  5. 05

    Document

    Capture decisions, changes, and what remains unresolved.

  6. 06

    Demonstrate

    Explain what was attempted and show evidence of the work completed.

✳ Prototype exploration is part of current student project support, but a completed prototype, equipment, supervision, timing, or specific outcome is not guaranteed. Confirm available support and scope before starting.
